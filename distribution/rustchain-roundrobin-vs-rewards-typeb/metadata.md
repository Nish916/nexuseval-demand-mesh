## Description
RustChain combines RIP-200’s 1-CPU-1-Vote round-robin model with Proof-of-Antiquity hardware attestation and antiquity-weighted rewards. This explainer separates network identity from reward weighting and shows why an antiquity multiplier is not a performance benchmark or guaranteed return.
